Modals & Popups: When you click something that opens a popup (like confirming an action), Moodle uses a default Bootstrap modal. You can round the modal corners to 16px, blur the background overlay (backdrop-filter: blur(5px)), and redesign the "Save/Cancel" buttons inside them.

Alerts & Toasts (Notifications): Moodle frequently shows green "Success" or red "Error" banners at the top of the screen. You can restyle these to look like floating, pill-shaped notifications (similar to macOS or Android notifications) with soft shadows.

Tooltips: The tiny black boxes that appear when you hover your mouse over an icon or button for a few seconds. You can soften their background, round the corners, and change the font.
